How to add page numbers

1

Add your PDFs

Click the select button or drop your files into the area provided. You can number several PDFs in one go, with the same settings.

2

Choose the layout

Pick single or facing pages, click one of the nine positions, set the margin, the first number and the pages to include.

3

Style the number

Choose the wording — number only, “Page {n}”, “Page {n} of {p}” or your own text — then the font, size, style and colors. The preview follows every change.

4

Number and download

Press “Add page numbers” and save each numbered PDF on its own, or all of them together in a single ZIP file.

Frequently asked questions

Is this page number tool really free?

Yes — completely. You can number as many PDFs as you like, several files at once, with every option unlocked. There is no subscription, no “premium” version and no sign-up: the whole tool runs for free in your browser.

Are my files uploaded to a server?

No. Your PDFs are opened, previewed and numbered entirely on your own device — nothing is sent anywhere. That is why the tool also works on sensitive documents: contracts, manuscripts and personal files never pass through a third-party server.

How do I leave the cover or the first pages unnumbered?

Two ways: switch to “Facing pages” and tick “First page is cover page” to skip just the cover, or use the “Pages to number” range to start numbering from any later page — for example from page 3, after a cover and a contents page. The “First number” still decides which number that first numbered page shows.

What do {n} and {p} mean in the text?

{n} is replaced by the number of the page being stamped, and {p} by the total number of pages in the file — so “Page {n} of {p}” prints “Page 3 of 12” on the third numbered page of a twelve-page file. Anything else you type in a custom text is printed as-is.

Can the numbers be removed again later?

The numbers are drawn onto each page, like ink printed on paper, so there is no one-click way to take them off the numbered copy. Keep your original file if you might need a clean version — you can always run the tool again on the original with different settings.
