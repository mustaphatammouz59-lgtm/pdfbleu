Your files stay on your device

PdfBleu runs entirely in your browser. When you use a tool — merge, split, rotate or image conversion — the processing happens on your phone or computer, thanks to a JavaScript library running locally.

  • We do not upload any file to a server.
  • We do not store, copy or keep any document.
  • We cannot see the content of your files: technically, they never leave your device.
  • No account and no sign-up are needed to use the tools.

Data we do not collect

We collect neither your name, nor your email address, nor the content of your documents through the site's tools. There is no sign-up form and no user database.

  • Google and its partners may use cookies to serve ads based on your visits to this site and other sites.
  • You can turn off personalized advertising in Google's ad settings.

Your browser may also store small pieces of technical information locally, needed for the pages to work. You can delete cookies at any time in your browser settings.
