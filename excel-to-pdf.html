How to convert an Excel spreadsheet to PDF

1

Choose your spreadsheet

Click the select button or drop your .xlsx file into the area provided. One workbook is all it takes.

2

Set the page options

Choose the page size and orientation, and decide whether each sheet should be fitted to the page width or spread across extra pages.

3

Download the PDF

Press “Convert to PDF”, then save your file: every sheet becomes clean PDF pages, ready to share or print.

Our Excel to PDF converter is completely free and works with no sign-up. Unlike classic services, your spreadsheet is never sent to a server: reading the .xlsx file and creating the PDF happen on your phone or computer, which protects your budgets, invoices, price lists and confidential figures.

The converter reads every sheet of your workbook — cell values and formulas as last saved by Excel, number formats such as percentages, currencies and dates, bold text, fill colors, borders and merged cells — then redraws the tables as printable PDF pages with selectable text. You control the page size, the orientation and whether wide tables are fitted to a single page width.
