How to organize a PDF

1

Add your PDFs

Click the select button or drop your files into the area provided. Every page of every file appears in one combined stream of thumbnails.

2

Arrange the pages

Drag a thumbnail to its new place, or use its buttons: ◀ ▶ nudge it, ⧉ duplicate it, ＋ slip a blank page in after it, ✕ remove it.

Frequently asked questions

Can I combine pages from several PDFs into one?

Yes — that is exactly what this tool is for. Add as many files as you like: all their pages live in one stream, and the download is a single PDF in precisely the order you arranged. The Mix button even interleaves the files page by page for you.

Does organizing change the quality of my pages?

No. Pages are copied from the original files exactly as they are — the text, images and vector drawings are never re-rendered, resampled or compressed. Only the order (and which pages are included) changes.

Can I add a blank page?

Yes. The ＋ button under any page inserts a blank right after it, and there is an “Add blank page” tile at the end of the grid. A blank takes the size of the page before it — or standard A4 when it comes first.

I deleted a page by mistake — can I get it back?

Yes. Removed pages are kept aside (the counter under the grid tells you how many), and sorting or pressing “Reset all” brings them back into the stream. Nothing is final until you press “Organize PDF”.

Are my files uploaded to a server?

No. Your PDFs are opened, rearranged and saved entirely on your own device — nothing is sent anywhere. That makes the tool safe for sensitive documents: contracts, ID scans and personal files never pass through a third-party server.
