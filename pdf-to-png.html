Frequently asked questions

PNG or JPG — which should I choose?

PNG is lossless: text, logos and diagrams stay perfectly sharp, but files are larger. JPG files are smaller and better for photos. For documents, PNG is usually the right choice.

How do I get my images?

A one-page PDF downloads as a single PNG. For several pages, all images are packed into one ZIP file, named by page number.

Why did the conversion stop on a huge document?

Rendering happens on your device, so very long documents at 3× can run out of memory on a phone. Lower the resolution to 1× or 2×, or split the PDF first and convert it in parts.
