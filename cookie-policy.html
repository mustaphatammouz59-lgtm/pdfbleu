What cookies are

Cookies are small text files that a website asks your browser to store on your device. They can be essential (needed for a page to work), functional (remembering a preference), or advertising-related (used to choose and measure ads).

Cookies on PdfBleu

  • The tools themselves need no cookies and no account. Processing happens in your browser and your files never leave your device.
  • Technical storage: your browser may keep small technical items (such as cached library files) so pages load faster. These contain no document content.
  • Advertising cookies (Google AdSense): this site may display ads provided by Google AdSense. Google and its partners may use cookies to serve ads based on your visits to this site and to other sites, and to measure ad performance.

Your choices

  • Personalized advertising: you can turn it off at any time in Google's Ad Settings. You can also learn more on aboutads.info.
  • Browser controls: every modern browser lets you block or delete cookies in its settings. Blocking cookies does not stop the PdfBleu tools from working.
  • Do Not Track: where your browser sends a Do Not Track signal, third-party advertising behavior is governed by those providers' own policies.

Third-party vendors

Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to users based on their visit to this site and/or other sites on the Internet. PdfBleu does not receive the content of these cookies and does not combine them with any personal data — we collect none.

Changes and contact
