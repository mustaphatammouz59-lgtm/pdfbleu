How to add a watermark

1

Add your PDFs

Click the select button or drop your files into the area provided. You can stamp several PDFs in one go, with the same watermark.

2

Design your watermark

Write your text and pick a font, size and color — or switch to “Place image” and use your logo or any PNG or JPG picture.

3

Place it your way

Choose one of nine positions or tile the page as a mosaic, then set the transparency, rotation, the pages to stamp and the layer. The preview updates as you go.

4

Stamp and download

Press “Add watermark” and save each stamped PDF on its own, or all of them together in a single ZIP file.

Frequently asked questions

Is this watermark tool really free?

Yes — completely. You can stamp as many PDFs as you like, several files at once, with every option unlocked. There is no subscription, no “premium” version of the watermark and no sign-up: the whole tool runs for free in your browser.

Are my files uploaded to a server?

No. Your PDFs are opened, previewed and stamped entirely on your own device — nothing is sent anywhere. That is why the tool also works on sensitive documents: contracts, payslips and personal files never pass through a third-party server.

Can a watermark added here be removed later?

A watermark drawn over the content becomes part of the page itself, so there is no one-click way to take it off, and a watermark placed below the content is buried even deeper, underneath everything else on the page. That said, no watermark is magic: a determined expert with specialist tools may still manage to remove or cover a stamp. Treat a watermark as a clear mark of ownership and a strong deterrent — not as encryption.

Should I use a text or an image watermark?

Text is the quickest way to mark ownership or status — a name, a company, “Draft” or “Confidential”. An image is better for a logo or an official-looking stamp, especially a PNG with a transparent background, which blends into the page without a white box around it.

Which fonts can I use for a text watermark?

Five familiar families — Arial/Helvetica, Impact, Verdana, Courier and Times New Roman — each matched to the closest built-in PDF font, so your watermark looks the same in every reader and adds almost nothing to the file size. Bold, italic and underline combine freely with the family you pick.
