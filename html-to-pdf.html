Frequently asked questions

Why did loading my URL fail?

Browsers only let one page read another site when that site explicitly allows it — a security rule known as CORS. Most large sites don't allow it, so the request is refused before the page ever reaches us. No tool running inside a web page can get around that, and we don't route your request through any third-party proxy. The reliable fix: open the page in your browser, save it or view its source, then paste the HTML or upload the saved .html file here.

Why does my PDF look different from the website?

For safety, scripts inside the page are removed and never run, so anything the page normally builds with JavaScript after loading may be missing. Images and fonts hosted on other domains appear only when those hosts allow them to be used elsewhere, and your login state, animations and hover effects can't be captured. What you see in the preview is exactly what ends up in the PDF.

Is the text in the PDF selectable?

No. The page is rendered as an image — like a very sharp screenshot — and placed into the PDF. It prints crisply, but you cannot select or copy the text. That is the trade-off of converting entirely in the browser, with no server involved.

Can it convert pages in Arabic or other scripts?

Yes. Your own browser draws the text, so Arabic, Hebrew, Chinese and other writing systems appear just as they do on screen — no missing characters and no question marks.

Is anything uploaded or stored?

No. The HTML you provide stays in this tab, the conversion runs on your device and the PDF is generated locally. Nothing is uploaded anywhere, and closing the tab erases everything.
