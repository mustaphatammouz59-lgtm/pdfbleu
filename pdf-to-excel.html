How to convert a PDF to Excel

1

Choose your PDF

Click the select button or drop your file into the area provided. One document is all you need.

2

Choose the layout

One sheet per page, or all pages combined into a single sheet. Then start the conversion.

3

Download the .xlsx

Press “Convert to Excel”, then open your workbook in Excel, LibreOffice Calc or Google Sheets.

Our PDF to Excel converter is completely free and works with no sign-up. Unlike typical services, your document is never uploaded to a server: table extraction happens on your phone or computer, which protects your invoices, statements and accounting documents.

The conversion suits PDFs with selectable text: invoices, quotes, statements, inventories or reports containing aligned tables. Amounts written in French format (for example 1 234,56 €) and percentages are recognized as real numbers in Excel, ready for your calculations and formulas. Scanned documents, which are really images, require character recognition (OCR): that feature is coming soon.
