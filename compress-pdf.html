How to compress a PDF

1

Choose your files

Click the select button or drop your PDFs into the area provided. You can add several at once.

2

Choose the level

Extreme compression for a very small file, recommended for a good balance, or low to preserve quality.

3

Compress and download

Press “Compress PDF”, compare the resulting sizes, then download each file or the full archive.

Our PDF compression tool is completely free and works with no sign-up and no file-count limit. Unlike typical services, your documents are never uploaded to a server: all processing happens on your phone or computer, which protects your contracts, invoices and personal documents.

An oversized PDF is often rejected by email inboxes or administrative platforms. By reducing the weight of your reports, homework or scanned documents, you make them easy to send and share, while choosing the trade-off between size and image quality yourself.
