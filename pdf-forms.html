Why use PdfBleu Forms

Private by design

Forms often hold your most personal details — names, addresses, tax numbers. Here, nothing is uploaded: the document is read, filled and rebuilt entirely in your browser.

✎

Real fields, real answers

Your answers are stored in the PDF's form fields, exactly like a dedicated form filler would save them — so the receiving office can read, copy and process them, unless you flatten the file.

🧩

Build forms from plain PDFs

Turn any plain document into a fillable form: text fields, checkboxes, radio groups, dropdowns and list boxes, with required flags, read-only fields and default answers.

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between saving and flattening?

A normal save writes your answers into the form fields: anyone opening the file can still change them, and the answers can be copied or imported like data. Flattening bakes the answers into the pages themselves and removes the fields — the file looks the same, but nothing can be edited or extracted as form data anymore. Flatten when the form is final; keep it unflattened when the other side still needs to work with your answers.

My document has no fields. What can I do?

Many "forms" are just printed lines and boxes with no real fields behind them. Switch to Edit Form mode and draw the fields yourself — it takes a minute, and the result is a genuinely fillable PDF. Automatic detection of blank spaces is not offered on purpose: guessing where answers belong would put fields in the wrong places.

How do I sign the form?

Signature fields can't be filled here. Save your form first, then open it in Sign PDF and place your signature on it. PdfBleu signatures are visual — drawn onto the page — not certified digital signatures.

Why does a government form show no fields?

Some official forms are built with XFA, a different form technology that lives inside the PDF alongside the pages. This tool reads standard PDF (AcroForm) fields; XFA fields can't be read or written here, so an XFA form shows an empty field list. Such forms usually need the issuing agency's own software or Adobe Acrobat.

Can I answer in Arabic or other non-Latin scripts?

Yes. Your answer is always saved exactly as typed. When a form's built-in font can't draw the characters, PdfBleu embeds a font that can, or flags the file so the reader renders the answers itself — your text is never replaced with question marks.

How do radio buttons work in Edit Form mode?

Radio buttons that share a group name form one question with several choices — the person filling the form can pick only one. Place a radio button, then use "Add another button" in its properties to add more choices to the same group, and give each button its own option value.
