Why crop with PdfBleu

✂️

An exact crop

The page size is set to your frame in the file itself — not a picture of the page. Text stays selectable, vectors stay sharp, and the document prints at exactly the new size.

📐

One frame, every page

Scanner margins, letterheads, footers: draw the frame once and apply it to the whole document, or give individual pages their own frame instead.

🔒

Private by design

The file is cropped in your browser and never uploaded. What you trim on your device stays on your device.

How to crop a PDF

1

Open your PDF

Click the select button or drop your PDF into the area provided. The document opens in the crop editor, right here in your browser.

2

Draw the frame

Drag over the area you want to keep. Move the frame by dragging inside it, resize it with the handles, or type exact values in points.

3

Choose the pages

Apply the same frame to all pages — the usual choice for trimming margins — or switch to current-page mode and crop pages one by one.

4

Crop and download

Press “Crop PDF”. The page sizes are updated in the file and your cropped document is ready to download.

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between “All pages” and “Current page”?

With “All pages”, one frame is applied to the whole document — ideal when every page carries the same margins, header or footer. With “Current page”, each page keeps its own frame: you draw it while that page is on screen, and pages you never touch stay exactly as they were.

My pages are not all the same size. What happens?

The frame is fitted inside each page it applies to. On a smaller page it is pulled in to the page edges; if the frame would cover a smaller page completely, that page is left uncropped — a crop that keeps everything is not a crop.

Does it work on rotated pages?

Yes. You crop what you see: draw the frame over the rotated page exactly as it appears on screen, and the tool converts your frame into the page's own coordinates, rotation included.

Will cropping make my file much smaller?

Barely. Cropping changes the page size but keeps the content, so the file size hardly moves. If shrinking the file is your goal, use Compress PDF afterwards.

I cropped too much — can I undo it?

Not from the cropped file: once the page boxes are set smaller, viewers show only the new size, and the surest way back is your original file — so keep it. (The full content does remain inside the cropped file, which is also why cropping must never be used to hide private information — use Redact PDF for that.)

Is the content outside the frame deleted?

No. It is hidden by the new page size, not removed from the file. Anything outside the frame can be brought back by enlarging the page boxes again with the right software. For permanent removal, Redact PDF rebuilds the document so the content is destroyed.
