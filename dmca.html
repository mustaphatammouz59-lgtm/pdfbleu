Our position

PdfBleu respects the intellectual property rights of others. Two facts shape how copyright works here:

  • PdfBleu hosts no user documents. Every tool runs in the visitor's own browser; files are never uploaded to, stored on, or distributed by our servers. There is therefore no user file for us to remove.
  • If you believe that content belonging to this site itself (a page, a text, an image, or a blog article) infringes your copyright, you can request its removal using the procedure below, in line with the U.S. Digital Millennium Copyright Act (17 U.S.C. §512) and equivalent laws.

How to send a removal request

Email contact@pdfbleu.com with the subject "DMCA removal request" and include:

  • Your full name, address, phone number and email (and the company you represent, if any).
  • A description of the copyrighted work you claim has been infringed.
  • The exact URL(s) on PdfBleu.com where the material appears.
  • A statement that you have a good-faith belief that the use is not authorized by the copyright owner, its agent, or the law.
  • A statement, under penalty of perjury, that the information in your request is accurate and that you are the copyright owner or authorized to act on their behalf.
  • Your physical or electronic signature.

What happens next

We review complete requests promptly and, where the claim is valid, remove or disable access to the material, normally within a few business days. Incomplete requests may be returned for the missing details.

Counter-notification

If your content was removed and you believe this was a mistake, email contact@pdfbleu.com with your details, the removed material and its former URL, and a statement of your good-faith belief that the removal was in error. We will review it under the same procedure.

Repeat infringers

Where applicable, accounts or contributors found to repeatedly infringe copyright lose access to the service.
