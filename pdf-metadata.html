Frequently asked questions

What is PDF metadata?

It is the document information stored inside the file: the title shown in a reader's tab, the author name, subject, keywords, and which program created or produced the PDF, with its dates. It is not printed on the pages, but anyone can read it in the file's properties.

Why clear metadata before sharing?

Files from Word, scanners and office software often carry your name, your company, or the original document title. Clearing the fields here removes that from the copy you send — the pages themselves stay identical.

Does editing metadata change my document?

No. Only the info fields change; pages, text, images and layout are preserved exactly, and your original file is never modified.
