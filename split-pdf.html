How to split a PDF

1

Choose your file

Click the select button or drop your PDF into the drop zone. One file is enough.

2

Choose your pages

Set custom or fixed ranges, or extract each page into a separate file.

3

Split and download

Press "Split PDF", then download each file or the full archive in a single click.

Our PDF splitter is completely free and works with no sign-up and no limits. Unlike typical services, your document is never uploaded to a server: all processing happens on your phone or computer, which protects your contracts, invoices and personal documents.

You can separate the chapters of a report, isolate an invoice in a statement, extract the pages of an assignment or share only the useful pages of a long document, without installing anything.
