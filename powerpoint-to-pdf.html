How to convert a PowerPoint presentation to PDF

1

Choose your presentation

Click the select button or drop your .pptx file into the area provided. One presentation is all it takes.

2

Start the conversion

Every slide is read and recreated as a PDF page: text, colored shapes, embedded images and simple tables, at the exact size of your presentation.

3

Download the PDF

Press “Convert to PDF”, then save your file: your slides become easy to share, send or print, even without PowerPoint.

Our PowerPoint to PDF converter is completely free and works with no sign-up. Unlike classic services, your presentation is never sent to a server: reading the .pptx file and creating the PDF happen on your phone or computer, which protects your talks, lessons, business presentations and confidential documents.

The converter reads the structure of every slide — titles, text boxes, bulleted lists, shapes, images and simple tables — then rebuilds the pages in PDF format with standard fonts, at the exact size of your slides. The result suits the vast majority of everyday presentations; advanced PowerPoint effects (animations, transitions, embedded charts, SmartArt) are, however, not reproduced, as noted above.
