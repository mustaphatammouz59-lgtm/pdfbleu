What PdfBleu is

PdfBleu is a free online toolbox for PDF files: merge, split, compress, convert, edit, sign, protect and understand your documents — directly in your web browser, on your phone or your computer.

PdfBleu is part of the Bleu family of free web tools, alongside CalcBlue (calculators) and other Bleu projects built on the same principles: free, simple, and respectful of the visitor.

Our difference: your files never leave your device

Most online PDF services ask you to upload your documents to their servers. PdfBleu was designed the other way around: every tool runs locally in your browser using JavaScript libraries loaded on the page. Your contracts, invoices, medical letters and family documents are processed on your own device and are never sent to us — technically, we could not see them even if we wanted to.

There is exactly one disclosed exception: the Translate PDF tool sends the extracted text of your document (never the file itself) to the MyMemory translation service, because translation cannot be done locally. This is stated clearly on that tool's page and in our privacy policy.

What you get

  • 36 free tools for organizing, converting, editing, securing and understanding PDFs.
  • No account, no sign-up, no limits set by us — processing speed depends only on your device.
  • No installation: everything works in the browser, mobile first.
  • Honest limits: where a tool has a technical limitation, the page itself tells you before you download.

How the site is funded

PdfBleu is free and is supported by advertising (Google AdSense), shown in clearly labelled spaces that never cover the tools or the download buttons. We never sell data — we do not collect any in the first place.
