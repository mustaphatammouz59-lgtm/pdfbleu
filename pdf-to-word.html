How to convert a PDF to Word

1

Choose your PDF

Click the select button or drop your file into the area provided. One document is all you need.

2

Start the conversion

The “No OCR” mode extracts the selectable text from your PDF and rebuilds the paragraphs and headings.

3

Download the .docx

Press “Convert to Word”, then download your document, editable in Word, LibreOffice or Google Docs.

Our PDF to Word converter is completely free and works with no sign-up. Unlike typical services, your document is never uploaded to a server: text extraction happens on your phone or computer, which protects your contracts, reports and personal documents.

The conversion suits documents with selectable text: reports, letters, homework, completed forms or administrative documents. Text, paragraphs, headings and simple formatting (bold, italics, sizes) are preserved in the .docx file produced. Scanned documents, which are really images, require character recognition (OCR): that feature is coming soon.
