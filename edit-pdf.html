How to edit a PDF

1

Open your PDF

Click the select button or drop your PDF into the area provided. All of its pages open in the editor, right here in your browser.

2

Add your content

Use the toolbar to add text, images, shapes, highlights or freehand drawings. Drag elements to move them, use the round handle to resize them, and fine-tune colors and sizes in the Properties panel.

3

Save your changes

Press “Save changes” and download your edited PDF. Your additions are drawn onto the pages, and the original document underneath stays untouched.

Our PDF editor is completely free and works with no sign-up. Unlike classic services, your document is never sent to a server: opening the PDF, placing your additions and building the edited file all happen on your phone or computer, which protects your contracts, forms, invoices and personal documents.

The editor is made for the edits people actually need every day: filling in a missing detail, adding a name or a date, placing a logo or a stamp image, highlighting an important passage, covering a wrong line with a whiteout box, or ticking a check box. What it deliberately does not promise is rewriting the text that is already inside the document — that kind of change needs the original source file, and sites that offer it online do it on their servers, with your document uploaded. Here, everything stays on your device.
