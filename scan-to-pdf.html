How to scan documents to PDF

1

Photograph each page

Open the camera on this page and take one photo per page — or add photos you already have from your gallery. Each photo becomes one page, in the order shown.

2

Arrange and enhance

Drag the cards into the right order, rotate any page that came out sideways, then choose Color, Grayscale or Black & white, plus the page size and margin.

3

Create and download

Press “Create PDF”, then download the merged document, each PDF separately, or all of them together in a single ZIP file.

PdfBleu scans and builds your PDF entirely on your own device: your documents are never uploaded to a server, never stored anywhere, and never seen by anyone else. That makes this scanner a safe choice for ID cards, signed forms, contracts and receipts — and it works even on a slow connection, because nothing has to travel over the network.

A phone camera is often the fastest scanner you own: photograph a signed contract before posting it, archive paper receipts and invoices, turn filled-in forms into one tidy file for an application, or save handwritten notes as a PDF you can share anywhere. With the Fit page size each page matches its photo exactly, while A4 and US Letter give you print-ready pages with the margin you choose.

Frequently asked questions

How does scanning work without an app?

Your browser can use your device camera directly. Tap “Use camera”, allow the permission, frame each page inside the green corners and tap “Take photo” once per page. When every page is captured, press “Create PDF” — the photos are combined into a PDF on the spot, on this page.

The camera will not open. What can I do?

Check that you allowed camera permission for this site in your browser settings, and that no other app is using the camera. If the live camera still is not available — some browsers and desktop computers limit it — use “Take a photo”, which opens your phone's camera app, or “Choose from gallery” for photos you already have. Both produce exactly the same pages.

What does the Black & white mode do?

It turns each page into pure black text on a white background, which makes printed documents crisper and the PDF smaller. The cut-off between black and white is chosen automatically for every page with Otsu's method, which finds the threshold that best separates the dark ink from the light paper in that photo. Grayscale keeps the shades in between, and Color keeps the photo as taken.

Are my scans uploaded anywhere?

No. The camera feed, the photos and the finished PDF all stay on your device — this page never sends them to a server. You can scan private documents, ID papers and contracts without them passing through anyone else's computer.

What do the page size and margin options mean?

“Fit” makes each PDF page exactly the size of its photo. A4 and US Letter place each photo, centred and scaled to fit, on a standard print page in the orientation you pick. The margin adds blank space around the photo on those standard pages — handy so nothing important sits right at the printer's edge.
