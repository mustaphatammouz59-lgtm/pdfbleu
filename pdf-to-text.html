Frequently asked questions

My PDF produced an empty text file — why?

Your PDF is most likely a scan: its pages are pictures of text, with no text layer to extract. Use our OCR PDF tool first to make it searchable, then extract again.

Is the layout preserved?

No — a .txt file is plain text. Lines and page order are kept, with a clear separator between pages, but columns, fonts and positions are not. If you need an editable formatted document, use PDF to Word instead.

Can I copy just part of the text?

Yes. After extracting, the full text appears in the box on this page: select what you need, or use the Copy button to take all of it.
