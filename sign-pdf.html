How to sign a PDF

Open your PDF

Frequently asked questions

Is this a digital signature?

Not in the certified sense. PdfBleu places a visual image of your signature onto the document and flattens it into the page — the same visible result you get from printing, signing and scanning, but cleaner. It does not embed a cryptographic certificate or a qualified timestamp, so for regulated documents that legally require a certified (qualified) electronic signature, use a certified signing service instead.

Is my signature stored anywhere?

No. Your signature is created in your browser and kept only in this page's memory so you can place it. It is never uploaded, never saved to your device by us, and it disappears completely when you close the tab.

What image formats can I upload for my signature?

PNG and JPG. A PNG with a transparent background works best, because it blends into the document without a white box around your signature. A photo of your signature on white paper also works — place it so the paper area sits on a blank part of the page.

Can I sign on my phone?

Yes. The whole tool runs in your mobile browser: draw your signature with your finger, drag the fields into place and download the signed PDF — no app to install.

Does it work on scanned PDFs?

Yes. Your signature is placed over the page exactly as it is displayed, so a scanned contract works just like a digital document — the signature simply lands on top of the scanned image.
