How to merge PDF files into one document

Sending five separate PDFs where one document was expected is one of the most common office frustrations: the signed contract in one file, its annexes in three more, and an ID scan floating on its own. Merging solves it — one clean file, pages in the order you choose, ready to email, upload to an administration portal, or archive. This guide shows how to do it in under a minute, free, and — unlike most online services — without uploading your documents to a server.

When merging is the right move

  • An application or tender asks for a single file with several documents inside.
  • You scanned a multi-page document as separate files and want it whole again.
  • You want one tidy archive file instead of a folder of fragments.
  • A report needs its annexes, receipts or certificates attached at the end.

Step by step

1. Open the Merge PDF tool

Go to the Merge PDF tool. It works on phones and computers, directly in the browser — nothing to install, no account to create.

2. Add your files

Drag and drop your PDFs into the drop zone, or tap the select button. You can add files one after another; each appears as a card in the list. Because the tool runs locally, even confidential documents never leave your device.

3. Put them in order

This is the step people skip — and regret. Drag the cards (or use the arrow buttons) until the list reads the way the final document should read: the first file in the list becomes the first pages of the merged PDF. A typical order: main document, then annexes, then supporting scans.

4. Merge and download

Press Merge PDF. The browser assembles the new document in seconds and downloads it. Open it once to check the page order before sending — a ten-second check that prevents most mistakes.

Tips for a clean result

  • Name files before merging (01-contract.pdf, 02-annex.pdf…) if you merge often — ordering becomes automatic.
  • Rotate sideways scans first with Rotate PDF, so no page appears upside down in the final file.
  • Remove blank or duplicate pages with Remove Pages instead of sending them along.
  • If the merged file is too heavy for an email or an upload form, run it through Compress PDF afterwards.
  • Password-protected files cannot be merged until they are unlocked; unlock your own documents first.

Common mistakes

Merging in the wrong order is the classic one — fix it before merging, because un-merging means starting over. Merging scans of different orientations produces a document that rotates wildly from page to page; normalize them first. And forgetting the original files: merging creates a new document and never changes the sources, so keep them until you have verified the result.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a limit to how many files I can merge?

PdfBleu sets no limit and requires no sign-up. Very large batches depend only on your device's memory, since everything is processed locally.

Does merging reduce quality?

No. Pages are copied exactly as they are — text, images and layout stay identical to the originals.

Are my files uploaded?

No. The merge happens entirely in your browser; your documents never leave your phone or computer.
