How to convert a PDF to PowerPoint

1

Choose your PDF

Click the select button or drop your file into the area provided. One document is all you need.

2

Start the conversion

Each page becomes a slide: the selectable text is placed in editable boxes, at its original position and size.

3

Download the .pptx

Press “Convert to PowerPoint”, then open your presentation in PowerPoint, LibreOffice Impress or Google Slides.

Our PDF to PowerPoint converter is completely free and works with no sign-up. Unlike typical services, your document is never uploaded to a server: text extraction happens on your phone or computer, which protects your presentations, reports and confidential documents.

The conversion suits PDFs with selectable text: exported presentations, reports, lessons or brochures. Each page yields a slide whose dimensions match the first page of the PDF, and each text line appears in its own editable box. Scanned documents, which are really images, require character recognition (OCR): that feature is coming soon.
